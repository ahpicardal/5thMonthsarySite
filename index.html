A Letter For You

My lab,

Five months. It somehow feels like time flew by, but at the same time, it feels like you've been part of my life for so much longer. Thank you for every laugh, every call, every random conversation, every small memory we've made, and even for the days when things weren't easy between us. Thank you for choosing me every single day.

Being far away from you is probably the hardest part of all of this. I won't pretend that the distance is easy or that I've gotten used to it, because I haven't. There are days when all I want is to be there with you instead of staring at a screen. But if these months have taught me anything, it's how sure I am about you. I don't choose you because you're close, because it's easy, or because everything is always perfect. I choose you because, no matter how apart we are, you're still the person I want to share my life with. Every day, I choose us on purpose.

I promise to keep trying to be someone who brings you comfort, peace, and love. I know I'm far from perfect, and I know I'll make mistakes along the way. But I hope you also know that I'll always be willing to learn, to listen, and to grow with you. Loving you isn't something I want to do only when it's easy. It's something I want to keep choosing every single day.

Thank you for being patient with the distance, the bad signal, the schedules that don't always match, and even the days when I'm quieter than usual. Thank you for making me feel like I always have someone to talk to. You're still the first person I want to tell about anything.

I'm really proud of what we've built in these five months. We've laughed together, cried together, grown together, and made memories that I'll always hold close to my heart. I know we're still at the beginning of our story, but that means there's still so much left for us to experience together.

Happy 5th monthsary, lab. Thank you for loving me, for believing in us, and for making these past five months some of the happiest of my life. I love you, always.

Yours,

— Adrian
